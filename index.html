The problem

Laundry facilities become crowded at peak times, and students cannot tell when a machine will be free.

How this portal solves it

It shows machine and slot availability for every day and lets students reserve and cancel slots, so laundry spreads out across the day instead of piling up at peak hours.

How it works

  1. Log inwith your registration number.
  2. Check the dashboardto spot peak hours.
  3. Book a free slotor let Book next free slot pick one.
  4. Track itBooked, In progress, Completed. Edit, cancel or delete anytime.
